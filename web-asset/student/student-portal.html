How to enter New Words

Follow this workflow for every entry:

  1. Choose a useful word or phrase from the article and select its part of speech.
  2. Enter the English word or phrase, the Vietnamese meaning, and a clear definition.
  3. For a monosyllabic word, write the word whole in syllabication and do not add stress.
  4. For a phrase, keep the spaces between words. For a hyphenated compound, keep the exact hyphen, such as air-strike.
  5. If a phrase contains a hyphenated multisyllabic word, split that word and mark one stressed syllable, such as in the MÓRN-ing.
  6. For extra points, split a multisyllabic compound part, such as air-con-di-tion-ing.
  7. Complete at least five entries, select Check, and correct any highlighted fields.

New Words Section

Read Instructions

Build your personal dictionary

New Words from your news reports are collected here. You can edit each entry, sort your list, and study from a clean dictionary-style version of your new academic vocabulary.

Follow this workflow for every entry:

  1. Copy a word or phrase you do not know from the article and paste it into Word/Phrase en.
  2. Use the three blue buttons to find the required information:
    • LD — Longman Dictionary: part of speech and English definition
    • GT — Google Translate: Vietnamese translation
    • WH — Word Helper: syllables and primary stress
  3. Enter syllables as follows:
    • One-syllable word: Type the whole word. Do not mark stress. Examples: book, straight, laugh
    • Multi-syllable word: Examples: ex-pec-TA-tion, RE-flex, in-STRUC-tions
    • Hyphenated compound A — all parts have one syllable: Keep the compound and its hyphens together. Examples: check-in, life-size, long-term
    • Hyphenated compound B — one or more parts have multiple syllables: Keep the hyphens, divide the multi-syllable parts into syllables, and capitalize the stressed syllables. Examples: thirty-seven → THIR-ty-SEV-en; runner-up → RUN-ner-up
    • Hyphenated phrase A — all words have one syllable: Type the whole phrase with its hyphens. Examples: step-by-step, down-to-earth
    • Hyphenated phrase B — one or more words have multiple syllables: Keep the hyphens, divide the multi-syllable words into syllables, and capitalize the stressed syllables. Examples: once-in-a-lifetime → once-in-a-LIFE-time; daughter-in-law → DAUGH-ter-in-law; middle-of-the-road → MID-dle-of-the-road; spur-of-the-moment → spur-of-the-MO-ment
  4. Click CHECK at any time to save your progress.
  5. When you have finished:
    • Click CHECK.
    • If SUBMIT turns green, click it to send your work.
    • If SUBMIT does not turn green, read the pop-up instructions, correct your entries, and click CHECK again.
