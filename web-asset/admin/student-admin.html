School setup needs attention

School year dates or quarter rows are missing or invalid. Grades, attendance periods, reports, and parent/student portal rollover dates cannot be trusted until this is corrected.

  1. Open School Setup and confirm the first and last school day.
  2. Click Auto-Fill Quarters, review the generated rows, and save.
  3. If the values were lost after a settings problem, run System Config Sync or contact an administrator.
